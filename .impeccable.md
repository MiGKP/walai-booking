## Design Context

### Users
- **Who they are**: Local tourists, families, and couples looking for a unique nature escape in Mahasarakham.
- **Job to be Done**: Booking a floating room ("ที่พักลอยน้ำ") and reserving kayaks easily.
- **Emotions**: Serenity, relaxation, luxury, connection with nature.

### Brand Personality
- **Voice & Tone**: Welcoming, calm, authentic, premium.
- **Three Words**: Organic, Heritage, Peaceful.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: "Isan Organic Oasis" (โอเอซิสธรรมชาติอีสานร่วมสมัย)
- **Colors**:
  - Base Background: #FDFCF7 (Warm Cream)
  - Primary: #123C30 (Deep Botanical Green)
  - Secondary: #4E878C (Serene Lagoon Teal)
  - Accent: #D9A05B (Warm Bamboo Gold)
  - Text: #2B2D2F (Soft Charcoal)
- **Typography**: `Pridi` for headings, `Sarabun` for body text.
- **Anti-references**: Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, vibrant blue gradients, stock-looking generic card shapes, glassmorphism, bounce animations, identical card grids, cyan-on-dark palettes.

### Design Principles
1. **Nature-First & Breathable**: Use spacious margins, generous whitespace, and natural colors. Let the layout breathe.
2. **Tactile & Organic**: Use thin stone-like borders, subtle wood/bamboo undertones, and soft organic curves instead of heavy box-shadows and rounded rectangles.
3. **Bespoke Animations**: Add purposeful micro-interactions — gentle water-ripple effects, smooth scroll reveals, staggered entrances. Never bounce or elastic easing.
4. **Editorial, Not Template**: Asymmetric layouts, varied spacing, left-aligned text. Break the grid intentionally for emphasis.
5. **Heritage & Authenticity**: Typography and visual language should honor the Thai cultural context and the natural beauty of the Walai Rukkhavej botanical garden.
